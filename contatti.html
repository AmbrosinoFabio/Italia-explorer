Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Data saved on your device

This website is a personal educational project and does not collect or send personal data to any server. Some preferences (dark mode, favorite cities, visited cities) are saved exclusively in your browser's localStorage : they stay on your device and you can delete them at any time by clearing your browsing data.

The contact form

This is an educational project without a server: the contact form does not actually send messages to anyone, it only shows a confirmation via JavaScript to demonstrate how it works.

External services used

To work, the website loads resources from some third-party services, which under their respective policies may receive your IP address during loading:

  • Google Fonts — for the "Poppins" font
  • OpenStreetMap / CARTO — for the interactive map
  • LoremFlickr — for some destination photos

Cookie

This website does not use profiling or advertising tracking cookies.
